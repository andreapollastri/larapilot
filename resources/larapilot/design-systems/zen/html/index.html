Zen — design system catalog

Black ink on white paper, and a great deal of space. For products that want to feel calm, exact and quiet: studios, makers, small shops, and any backoffice that should get out of the way.

Principles

Four

Black and white only

No accent, no red or green, no gradients, no shadows. Greys only for secondary text and hairlines.

Ma

Empty space is the material. An 8 px scale, wide margins, few things per screen. When in doubt, remove.

Status by shape

Filled, ring, half, cross — always with a word. An error is a 2 px ink rule, an icon and the text.

One ensō

A single brush circle, the only ornament: the logo, empty states, the 404, the sign-in side.

Type

Sans · serif · mono

Sans · 300 headings · 400 body · 500 labels

Aa 12

Inter, Helvetica Neue, system-ui. Body at 14 px, labels at 11 px uppercase.

Serif · rare display lines

Ma

Iowan Old Style, Hiragino Mincho. The hero, the story, a quote, the sign-in line.

Mono · codes

K-1048

ui-monospace. Order numbers, kbd, file types. Figures elsewhere use tabular sans.
