Questions, answered

Can my clients pay without creating an account?

Yes. Every invoice has its own secure page with card, Apple Pay, Google Pay and SEPA transfer. Your client never signs up for anything.

What does Fjord cost when a client pays by card?

1.4% + €0.25 for European cards, passed on at cost. Bank transfers are free.

Can I bring my invoice numbers from another tool?

Import a CSV from Harvest, FreshBooks or a spreadsheet, and Fjord continues your sequence from the last number.

Where is my data stored?

In Frankfurt and Stockholm, encrypted at rest. You can export everything, or delete it, at any time.
