Prose, drop cap, two columns

Mulberry Trattoria opened in 1987 in a former butcher’s shop at 156 Mulberry Street, and the marble counter where the scales once stood is still where the antipasti are plated. Tonight the book shows ninety covers on the first turn and seventy-eight on the second, with a party of twelve in the back room for a seventieth birthday.

The kitchen runs on the same three sauces it always has. What changed is the book: every reservation for the five restaurants now lives in one place, and the host stand on Mulberry can see when the Grill on Grand Street is full and send a walk-in two blocks east instead of away.
