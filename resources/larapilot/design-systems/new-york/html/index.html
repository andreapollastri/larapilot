The front page

Didone headlines, signage bands and one taxi-yellow accent

For restaurants, hotels, venues, publishers and any product that wants a confident, metropolitan voice.

Newsprint off-white, ink black and a single accent — taxi #F7B500. Headlines and big numbers are set in a high-contrast Didone; signage and interface in Helvetica, white on black under a thin white stripe; long reads in a text serif. Every screen links one stylesheet, ../tokens.css, and follows the operating system’s theme; dashboard-dark.html forces the night edition.

The two rules and the five restaurants

Rule No. 1

The taxi rule

Taxi yellow is a surface under ink — the main button, the active nav item, a selected row, a highlighter. It is never text, an icon or a thin line on newsprint. On black it may be ink too.

Ink on taxi 10.4:1Taxi on black 10.3:1

Rule No. 2

The line rule

The five line colours name restaurants and chart series — never a status. A bullet always carries its letter, so colour is never the only cue.
