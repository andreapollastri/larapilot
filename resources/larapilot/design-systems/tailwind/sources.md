Version targeted: **Tailwind CSS v4.3** (values taken from the `tailwindcss@4.3.3` npm package, October
2026). Icons: **Heroicons v2** (MIT). Sample content (Tidewater, Lumenfield and their customers) is
fictional.

## What was left out on purpose

- Colour families the screens do not use (slate, zinc, stone, orange, amber, lime, emerald, teal, cyan,
  blue, violet, purple, fuchsia, pink, rose, neutral, mauve, olive, mist, taupe): add them from theme.css when needed.
- `2xl:` breakpoint utilities, container queries, 3D transforms, animations beyond `spin`/`pulse`
  variables: no packaged screen needs them.
