Frequently asked questions

Does Tidewater change anything in our Stripe account?

No. We ask for a restricted, read-only key. Tidewater never creates, edits or refunds anything, and you can revoke the key at any time from your Stripe dashboard.

How far back does the history go?

All of it. The first import replays every invoice since your first customer, usually in under ten minutes for 100,000 invoices.

What counts toward the MRR limit of my plan?

Your average MRR over the last three months. A one-off spike never moves you up a tier.

Can we keep our data in the EU?

Yes. Choose Frankfurt when you create the workspace; data, backups and support access stay in the EU.
