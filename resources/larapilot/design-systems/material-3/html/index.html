Larapilot design system · material-3

Meadow Field, a field-service app drawn in Material 3

Angular Material (M3 theme), Vuetify 3 and Material Web screens share these tokens: one seed colour, #4C7A34, expanded with SchemeTonalSpot into every --md-sys-color-* role, light and dark. Copy tokens.css next to a mockup as material-3-tokens.css, put md3-mockup on <body>, and start from the closest screen below.
