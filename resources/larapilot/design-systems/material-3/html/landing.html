FAQ

Questions, answered

Something else? Write to us and a person replies within a working day.

Does the mobile app work without signal?

Yes. Technicians download the day’s jobs in the morning; checklists, photos and signatures are stored on the phone and sync when it reconnects.

Can we import jobs from our current tool?

Import customers, sites and open jobs from CSV, or connect QuickBooks and Xero for invoices.

Who counts as a technician?

Anyone who is assigned jobs. Dispatchers, managers and office staff are free.

Where is our data stored?

In the United States or the European Union, chosen when the workspace is created.
