Questions

Good to know.

Still unsure? Text the front desk at (760) 555-0142. A real human answers, usually from the sand.

KNLTMRReplies in about 4 minutes
I can’t swim very well. Can I still learn?

Yes. Beginner lessons stay in waist-deep water and you wear a leash the whole time. You need to be comfortable putting your face in the water, that’s it.

What if the waves are flat or stormy?

We make the call at 5:30 am. If it’s unsafe or flat, you get a free reschedule or a full refund, your choice.

How old do kids need to be?

Kids camps are for ages 7 to 12. Teens 13+ join group lessons with a parent’s waiver.

Can I pause the Sunday Club?

Pause up to two months a year from your account, no fee. Unused lessons roll over one month.
