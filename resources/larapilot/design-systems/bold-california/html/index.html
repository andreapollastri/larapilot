SundaySurf Co.

BOLD California

An original Larapilot design system: sun-drenched, optimistic, confident. Heavy display type, sunset and Pacific colours on warm sand, pill buttons and sticker shadows, for a public site and its back office. Sample product: Sunday Surf Co., a surf school with rentals and a shop in Encinitas. Every screen links one stylesheet, ../tokens.css, follows the OS theme (warm night in dark), and uses bc- classes documented in components.md.
