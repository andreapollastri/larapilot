02

Six theses

Written on the studio wall in 2014, edited twice since. We still argue about number five.

  1. § 01

    The grid is a promise, not a cage.

    Twelve columns, one baseline, rules you can see. Then we break exactly one of them, on purpose.

  2. § 02

    One colour is enough if it means something.

    Ink, paper, concrete — and a single signal for the thing you must not miss.

  3. § 03

    Every poster is also a room.

    We design the flyer and the door, the catalogue and the wall it hangs on. Same hands, same rules.

  4. § 04

    Legible at 3 a.m.

    Big type, real contrast, no clever grey-on-grey. If the bouncer can't read it, it doesn't ship.

  5. § 05

    The city is the client.

    Small budgets, long relationships, public spaces. We say no to work that makes Berlin more expensive and less strange.

  6. § 06

    Nothing is finished until it's used.

    We open our own rooms to test what we make — the venue is the studio's longest-running prototype.
