Pulseboard Larapilot design system · modern · prefix mo-

Modern — crisp, quick, a little electric.

An original system in the spirit of 2025–2026 product design: hairline borders, one violet accent, glass and glow used sparingly, bento layouts, ⌘K and keyboard hints everywhere. Dark-first with an equally careful light theme; every screen follows the OS, dashboard-dark.html forces dark. Sample product: Pulseboard, product analytics for Acme Inc.
