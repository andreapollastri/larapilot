Questions

What counts as an event?

Any call to track(), a page view or an identify. Session replay frames and internal traffic you exclude are free.

What happens if I go over my plan?

Nothing breaks. We keep ingesting, email the owner at 80% and 100%, and bill overage at €12 per extra million at the end of the month.

Can I host my data in the EU?

Yes, on every plan. Pick Frankfurt or Oregon when you create a workspace; data never leaves the region.

Do you offer discounts for startups and non-profits?

Companies under two years old with less than $5M raised get Growth free for a year. Non-profits get 50% off forever.
