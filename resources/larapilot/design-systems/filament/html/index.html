Filament v4 · mockup kit

Northwind Ops — screen catalog

Static, offline replicas of a Filament 4 panel (Filament 3 compatible) for Northwind Ops, a wholesale coffee and kitchen-goods distributor. Every screen links ../tokens.css only; copy it as filament-tokens.css and keep body.fi-mockup. Light and dark follow the OS; <html class="dark"> forces dark.
